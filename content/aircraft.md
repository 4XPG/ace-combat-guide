## Aircraft Stats Description
- Power: Acceleration
- Stability: Minimum speed before the aircraft enters stall, the higher the value the lower
- Offense: Missile attack power
- Defense: Durability, how many hits the plane can sustain before going down
- Mobility: Turn rate

## Range Cheat Sheet
- Gun maximum range: 450ft
- Missile lock maximum range: 1000ft
- Optimum turn speed for most aircraft: around 680-700mph

## Remarks
- Player's hangar only can store up to 8 aircraft, any excess aircraft must be sold before buying a new one
- Every time the player gets shot down/crashes the aircraft will be permanently removed from the hangar. Losing the last available aircraft will result in game over
- Starting fuel amount varies between mission
- Unlike later installments, every aircraft carry 9999 gun ammo and 65 missiles regardless of stats. This is compensated by having missile attack power determined by aircraft's Offense stats.
- Much like other Ace Combat titles before Ace Combat 04, player's missile behaves akin to SAAM. After launching missile the player must maintain lock within the steering circle to achieve higher accuracy, otherwise the missile may lose track easily.
