## General Mission Guides
- Enemy SAM can sometimes redirect at the player. Whenever missile alert goes off, immediately back off from SAM kill radius to avoid its missile from retrack player aircraft. Enemy SAM can also take a potshot without locking on to player first.
- Hard difficulty also raises all enemy fighter AI skill by to maximum (Ace) on top of frequently adding new set of enemies or replacing certain enemy units with more dangerous counterpart
- On ravine flight missions throttle control is disabled. While this is likely intended to prevent player aircraft from stalling, it also renders faster aircraft useless for ravine flight.
- Stealth fighters and bombers are completely invisible to player's radar until the player approaches them up to Mid radar range (around 1500m)
